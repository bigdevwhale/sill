# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

The first version.

- A seed catalogue of 121 packets in eight sections, printed like lithographs, with the back of the packet: depth, germination, waterings to bloom, when to sow, neighbours and a first step.
- Five pots on a windowsill; sowing is trying, watering is going back, the bloom moves out into the garden beyond the window.
- Eight kinds of plants drawn in code, each grown from its own number, through seed leaves, true leaves, buds and bloom; thirst makes them droop, without notifications.
- Compost that makes the next planting quicker, and a vase for dried flowers.
- The seed tin: a wind seed each week from the section you've sown least, self-sown neighbours of blooms, packets put aside.
- Gifts to friends as plain text with a sticky note; shared back in, they land in the tin.
- Hand-written packets, and the year's herbarium.
- Photos with the phone's own camera. English and Russian. No internet.
